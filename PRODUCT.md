# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BeeWise primarily serves parents in Vietnam using an Android phone to find a tutor for their child. Tutors are a secondary user group who can submit a profile for staff review; an application is not a public tutor profile until staff/backend verification. The primary persona is Chị Lan, a parent looking for a suitable math tutor while balancing tutor qualifications, tuition, and schedule. The other Lab 2 personas represent parents who need help expressing their search, comparing cost and availability, or understanding each onboarding step. These personas are design assumptions, not validated user research.

## Product Purpose

Help parents discover and evaluate tutors, understand qualifications, price and availability, and request a supported connection through a clear process with a BeeWise consultant. The app also guides parent, tutor, and consultant through trial lesson, class confirmation, payment, and the official schedule.

The proposed Lab 2 success criterion is that at least four of five parents starting as guests can find a suitable tutor, review price and availability, and submit a connection request within five minutes without assistance or a critical error. This is a proposed usability target, not a measured result.

## Brand Personality

Tin cậy, ấm áp, rõ ràng. Use calm, respectful Vietnamese copy; explain what happens next and why information is needed; make fees, identity/verification status, workflow state, and errors easy to understand.

## Anti-references

No named reference product or visual anti-reference was supplied. Treat the Lab 2 Figma Final UI, Design System, Components pages, and `handoff/flutter-handoff.md` as the design source of truth. Avoid unsupported tutor claims, invented fees or payment instructions, unclear workflow transitions, and visual differences that are not recorded in `docs/design-traceability.md` and synchronized to Figma.

## Design Principles

1. Make tutor qualifications, cost, and availability comparable before asking a parent to commit.
2. Explain each step in the connection and onboarding process, including the party responsible for the next action.
3. Preserve a parent's selected tutor, entered details, and place in a flow through authentication and recoverable errors.
4. Let only verified service outcomes advance connection, payment, and schedule states.
5. Keep every screen readable, accessible, and consistent with the Lab 2 Figma design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the Lab 2 handoff: support 360dp and 412dp layouts, text scaling and Vietnamese wrapping; keep body text at least 14sp; make interactive targets at least 48dp; preserve 4.5:1 normal-text contrast; pair status colors with text/icons; support TalkBack semantics, safe areas, and keyboard insets. Persona-specific access needs are design assumptions to validate, not medical claims.
